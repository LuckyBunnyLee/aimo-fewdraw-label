## What we changed

The released records do not contain DeepMathGAP problem text, answers or our rewrites of them. They hold derived values only:
- DeepMathGAP problem ids;
- per-draw fields: version family, wave, replicate, answer status (numeric, truncated, abstain or invalid), correctness and output-token count, for answers generated by Qwen3.5-4B;
- per-problem summaries, labels and statistics computed from those fields.
